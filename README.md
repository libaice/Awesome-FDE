## What-is-a-forward-deployed-engineer

A **Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE)** is an engineer who works close to customers, often inside or alongside customer teams, to build, integrate, deploy, and iterate on technical solutions in real-world environments. The role is most famously associated with **Palantir**, where Forward Deployed Software Engineers helped deploy Gotham and Foundry into complex government and enterprise workflows. In the AI era, companies like **OpenAI**, **Anthropic**, **Scale AI**, and many enterprise AI startups are adopting similar models to help customers turn frontier AI capabilities into production systems.



Unlike a traditional **Software Engineer**, an FDE does not only work from a predefined ticket queue. Unlike a traditional **Solutions Engineer**, an FDE usually writes production code, designs system architecture, debugs data and integration issues, and owns delivery outcomes. The role lives at the intersection of engineering, customer delivery, and product feedback.
